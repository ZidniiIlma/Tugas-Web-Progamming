About the song

The song's Spanish title, "Viva la Vida", is taken from a painting by 20th-century Mexican artist Frida Kahlo. In Spanish, viva translates to "long live", so "Long Live Life" is an accurate translation and the painting reflects the artistic irony of acclaiming life while suffering physically.
